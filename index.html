About this Tool

Welcome to the unofficial ARC Raiders Loot Tracker. This database is updated for Patch 1.7.0 and helps you manage your inventory.

  • Recycle Guide: Find out which items yield ARC Alloy or Powercells.
  • Quest Items: Never sell items needed for "Flickering Flames" or other quests.
  • Values: Compare sell prices vs. crafting utility.

This is a fan project and not affiliated with Embark Studios.
